# Code of conduct

AutoFlowMate is for everyone, including people who are new to automation and to open source.

- Be respectful, patient and helpful. Assume good intentions.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks, in any language.
- Don't share other people's private information.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who
break these rules. To report a problem privately, use the contact on the maintainer's GitHub
profile.
